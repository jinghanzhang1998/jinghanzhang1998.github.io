I am **Jinghan Zhang**, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at **Clemson University** (expected 2027), advised by [Dr. Kunpeng Liu](https://www.kunpengliu.com/) in the **CLAIM Lab**.

My research interests include **human-inspired AI, collaborative AI, inclusive and adaptive AI, natural language processing, large language models, reinforcement learning, data-centric AI, and AI for scientific discovery and social good**.

My current work focuses on three connected goals:

1. Helping AI systems learn from human intelligence and extend it toward more capable problem-solving and discovery.
2. Enabling AI systems to accumulate and reuse experience for continual improvement.
3. Adapting AI capabilities across users, domains, and environments for reliable and inclusive access.

I welcome collaborations and interdisciplinary conversations that advance reliable, inclusive, and human-centered AI. Please feel free to [contact me](/contact/).
