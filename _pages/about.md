I am **Jinghan Zhang**, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at **Clemson University** (expected 2027), advised by [Dr. Kunpeng Liu](https://www.kunpengliu.com/) in the **CLAIM Lab**.

## My Research Interests

My research interests span **Large Language Models (LLMs), Human-Inspired AI, Collaborative AI, Inclusive and Adaptive AI, and Data-Centric AI**. I study how AI systems can learn generalizable principles from human intelligence, improve continually through collaborative experience, and adapt their knowledge, behavior, and accessibility across users, domains, and environments.

## My Current Work

My current work is organized around three connected directions:

1. **Human-Inspired AI:** I develop methods that translate human principles—such as evidence-guided search, exploration, and evaluative judgment—into computational mechanisms for reliable complex reasoning and behavior optimization.
2. **Collaborative AI:** I study how AI systems can learn continuously from limited feedback, diverse interaction signals, and collaborative experience with people and other systems.
3. **Inclusive and Adaptive AI:** I build methods that acquire domain expertise, evaluate evidence and risk, and transfer existing AI capabilities across domains, users, and accessible representations.

Together, these directions support my broader vision of **Inspired AI**: systems that proactively discover useful knowledge, learn from experience, respond responsibly to new evidence, and adapt their capabilities for scientific discovery and broad access.

I welcome collaborations and interdisciplinary conversations that advance reliable, inclusive, and human-centered AI. Please feel free to [contact me](/contact/).
